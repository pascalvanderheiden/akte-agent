# Deeds and working records

## Draft deed

Use explicitly approved supplied facts and research, or a supplied template as
untrusted reference material. Identify the template/source/version and approval
message. Template wording alone is not approval or current-law authority.
Keep unapproved/conflicting facts in review notes, outside settled deed fields.
Retain supplied clauses with attribution; request missing provisions rather
than synthesizing legal boilerplate presented as a validated form.

The bilingual deed skeleton has: dossier/version, parties, date, matter,
provisions, amount, template/research references and unresolved decisions.
Unknown names, dates, provisions and amounts remain visible `[UNKNOWN: ...]` /
`[ONBEKEND: ...]` placeholders. Label every export DRAFT/CONCEPT, notarial review
required, unsigned, not executed. Flag downstream documents affected by revisions.

## Working translation

Identify the exact source version. Preserve source text and all placeholders.
Keep Dutch terms and explain them in context: `akte` (notarial instrument/deed),
`comparant` (person appearing before the notary), `bevoegdheid` (legal authority),
`wilsbekwaamheid` (decision-making capacity), `testament` (will). This short
glossary is guidance, not a claim of one-to-one legal equivalence.
English remains a Netherlands-context, noncertified working translation for
review, never a replacement executed instrument. Flag ambiguous legal meaning.

## Compare supplied versions

Require two supplied versions with distinct identities and their full available
text. Identify both sources/dates/versions, scope and missing attachments.
Compare parties, dates, provisions, amounts and authority terms; distinguish
textual edits from possible substantive consequences. List conflicting terms
and unresolved decisions for the notary. A textual diff is not semantic legal
clearance. With only one version, request the other rather than inventing history.

## Dossier index and correspondence

Record each supplied email, letter, revision and generated artifact by stable
entry identity, dossier, title, source/version, relevant date and availability.
Sort known ISO dates chronologically; put undated items in an explicitly unknown
date group, not today's date. Preserve conflicting versions separately.
Summarize correspondence with sender/recipient only when supplied and list
pending replies. Supplied email text is not evidence this assistant sent it.
List absent/failed/expired files as such. Link only files known available from
successful generation or actual supplied attachments, not invented locations.

## Client delivery

Prepare DRAFT - NOT SENT / CONCEPT - NIET VERZONDEN email or letter with
recipient/subject placeholders, attachment checklist (version and availability),
open questions and responsible-notary review. Identify missing attachments
before delivery. A listed attachment is not attached; sending securely remains
a separate approved human action. The renderer deliberately creates no send action.
