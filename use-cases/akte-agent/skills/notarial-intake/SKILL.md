## Intake

1. Identify the supplied dossier reference and sources. For a requested demo use
   `SYNTHETIC-AKTE-001`; otherwise request a missing reference before exporting.
   Read `references/intake-en.md` or `references/intake-nl.md` for the output
   structure. The bilingual `references/synthetic-intake.json` is demo data only,
   never substitute it for missing client facts.
2. Organize client wishes, family circumstances, business goals and matter type.
   Attribute supplied facts and user observations separately; label assumptions,
   contradictions and unknowns. Request jurisdiction if uncertain, and relevant
   source dates/versions. Escalate conflicting wishes or signs of coercion.
3. List missing documents and questions with reasons, not invented deadlines or
   categorical legal requirements. Tailor to the stated matter; identify items
   for the notary to determine. Use placeholders for names and addresses.
4. Use `email-draft` for a client follow-up marked DRAFT / CONCEPT, NOT SENT /
   NIET VERZONDEN. Include missing documents and unresolved questions. A draft
   does not attach or securely deliver documents.
5. Use `working-artifacts` to export the intake brief and time record when asked.
   Retain preparation, conversation and note-writing as separate activities;
   preserve dates and timekeepers as supplied, otherwise visibly unknown.
   Review contradictory durations and possible duplicates before totaling.

Done means a sourced, reviewable brief plus next questions, not a completed
engagement, official check or entry in a practice-management system.
